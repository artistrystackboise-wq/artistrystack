Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

The Artistry Stack ("we," "us," or "I") provides website, digital intake form, and inventory tracking setup for independent beauty professionals in Boise and the Treasure Valley. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, fill out one of our forms, or respond to one of our ads, and how that information is used.

Information we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us, plus basic technical data from your browser:

  • Contact details: your name, email address, and phone number.
  • Business details: the services you're interested in and anything you share about your business (for example, your booking software or current setup).
  • Technical data: information such as browser type, device, pages visited, and how you arrived at our site (for example, from a Google ad), collected through cookies and similar tools.

We collect this through our website, our intake form, and lead forms in our Google ads.

How we use your information

  • To contact you about the services you asked about and answer your questions.
  • To prepare quotes, proposals, and project details.
  • To deliver and support services you purchase.
  • To understand how people find our website and improve our ads and site.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

How information is shared

We share information only with trusted service providers who help us run the business, such as:

  • Google: Google Ads (including ad lead forms), Google Forms, and related tools that collect and store form responses and measure ad performance.
  • Website hosting providers that keep this site online.

We may also disclose information if required by law.

Cookies and advertising

This site may use cookies and the Google tag to measure visits and ad conversions (for example, when someone clicks "Get Started"). Google may use this data as described in How Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can manage ad personalization at Google Ad Settings or block cookies in your browser settings.

How long we keep information

We keep your information only as long as needed to respond to you, provide services, and meet business and legal recordkeeping needs. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Your choices

  • Ask to see, correct, or delete the information we have about you.
  • Opt out of follow-up messages at any time by replying "stop" or letting us know.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, including secure (HTTPS) connections and reputable, secure service providers. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take care to protect what you share with us.

Children's privacy

Our services are for business owners and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
